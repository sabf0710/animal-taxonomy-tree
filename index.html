    What is This?

    An overview tree of animal taxonomy built to make scientific names and relationships easy to browse without needing to hover for context.

    What for?

    Taxonomic classification isn't as settled as most charts tend to imply. Sources disagree on how many classes and phyla exist in Animalia; the number of animal classes alone ranges from 100 to 107 depending on the source, as new genetic evidence reshapes old groupings.

    This chart doesn't try to resolve that disagreement. Instead, Catalogue of Life and Encyclopedia of Life are used as primary references, with every taxon cited individually. Images sit on the card itself, so you're not left guessing what Cetartiodactyla looks like.
